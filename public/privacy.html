Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Quasar ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and use your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Data: We collect your email address when you create an account via Firebase Authentication.
  • Health Data: We collect body statistics (weight, height, age, gender) and meal logs to calculate your personalized calorie targets.
  • Usage Data: We collect basic information about how you interact with the app to improve our algorithms.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected data solely to:

  • Provide personalized calorie and macronutrient targets.
  • Maintain your weight progress and meal history.
  • Improve the accuracy of our metabolic calculations.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase. We do not sell your personal or health data to third parties. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

4. Your Rights

You may request to delete your account and all associated data at any time through the app settings or by contacting us.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at supportquaser@gmail.com.
