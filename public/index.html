Thermic Engine

Quasar constantly calculates your actual Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) based on your weight inputs and calorie logs, adjusting your targets automatically to prevent stalls.

Deficit Cycling

Prevent metabolic adaptation. Quasar dynamically cycles your calories and incorporates planned diet breaks to keep your hormones optimized and fat loss consistent.

Load Management

Injured or taking a deload week? Log your load status and Quasar will instantly adjust your caloric targets to match your reduced activity levels, preventing unwanted weight gain.

Using advanced Naive Bayes classification, Quasar analyzes your qualitative check-in responses to provide highly personalized, actionable suggestions to keep your fitness journey on track.

🥩

Protein Streaks

Consistency is key. Build and track your daily protein adherence streaks. Hit your target to keep the fire alive and maximize your muscle retention.

📊

Dynamic Macros

Every weekly check-in, Quasar automatically recalculates your precise macro splits (Proteins, Fats, Carbs) based on your chosen aggressive or steady trajectory.
