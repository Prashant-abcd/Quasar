Account Deletion Request

We're sorry to see you go! At Quasar, we respect your privacy and your right to control your data.

How to delete your account and data:

Option 1: Full Account Deletion (Inside the app)
You can delete your account and all associated data instantly at any time by pressing the Logout button in the top right corner of the dashboard, and then tapping the Trash Bin icon on the confirmation screen.

Option 2: Partial Data Deletion (Via Email Request)
If you want to keep your account but delete specific pieces of data (such as specific weight logs, meal history, or profile stats), you can request a manual partial deletion by emailing our support team. Please specify exactly what you want deleted and provide the email address you registered with.

  • Data Deleted: Your profile, email address, weight logs, and daily meal/macro data will be permanently wiped from our secure database.
  • Data Kept: No personal or health data is retained after deletion.
